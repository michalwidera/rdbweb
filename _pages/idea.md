This is not about IDEA from JetBrains &mdash; though they did help, and
then stopped.

Some ideas are worth spreading. Some shouldn't be. When we encounter the
first difficulties, we usually reject ideas that are hard to understand
from the beginning.

Building this system took a couple of years and still attracts me. The
lonely path of discovery was fascinating and addictive. I hope you find
beauty in this solution just as I did &mdash; in the algebra, not just
the code.

And yes &mdash; this work is for people. Free of charge.

I hope my effort finds a use case someday. The ideal situation would be
if someone let me work on the system and allowed me to earn money from
this work. At the moment, though, it's rewarded with satisfaction and a
deep conviction of future need. The exchange rate for that currency is
undefined, but I think it has its own value.
