      <span class="sec-eyebrow">Word mark</span>
      <h2>Retractor<span style="color: #6699FF">DB</span>, one word, no space</h2>
      <p class="body">
        <strong>Retractor</strong> starts with a capital letter; <strong>DB</strong>
        is always capitals. It&rsquo;s correct to use a single word in the text:
        RetractorDB without spaces or dashes. If you want to use the color form,
        you must follow certain rules. If font is selected &mdash; it is set in
        <strong>Ubuntu Italic</strong>. &ldquo;Retractor&rdquo; is black on a light
        plane, white on a dark plane. &ldquo;DB&rdquo; is always baby blue,
        <code>#6699FF</code>.
      </p>

    <div class="home-in">
      <span class="sec-eyebrow">Device mark</span>
      <h2>The scissors: a de-interleave, drawn</h2>
      <p class="body">
        The primary graphic mark is the <strong>scissors / &ldquo;cut-here&rdquo;</strong>
        lockup &mdash; a literal drawing of the <strong>de-interleave operator</strong>.
        The mark is distributed as SVG in two ink variants &mdash; black for light
        backgrounds, white for dark backgrounds &mdash; so it stays crisp at any
        size. The pivot ring is always baby blue, <code>#6699FF</code>, in both.
      </p>

    <div class="home-in">
      <span class="sec-eyebrow">Usage</span>
      <h2>Four rules for the marks</h2>
      <ol class="steps" style="grid-template-columns: repeat(auto-fit, minmax(220px, 1fr))">
        <li><span>01</span><div>Don&rsquo;t recolor the device mark outside black / white / #6699FF.</div></li>
        <li><span>02</span><div>Don&rsquo;t distort, rotate, or add effects (shadows, gradients, outlines).</div></li>
        <li><span>03</span><div>Keep clear space around the mark of at least one mark-width on every side.</div></li>
        <li><span>04</span><div>Use <code>*-onblack.svg</code> (word mark) and <code>*-dark.svg</code> (device mark) on dark surfaces; don&rsquo;t place the light variant on a dark background or vice versa.</div></li>
      </ol>
